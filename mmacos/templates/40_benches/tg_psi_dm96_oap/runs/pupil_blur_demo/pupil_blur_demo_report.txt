---- the lesson ----
blur-free floor (sigma=0, intrinsic Nyquist regularization at lambda=0.050): checker 8.1%, random 3.6%
a CALIBRATED read holds near that floor until the blur doubles it: checker 2x floor at sigma = 0.40 pitch; the NAIVE read doubles the floor by sigma = 0.20 pitch
the structural knee is MTF(actuator Nyquist) = lambda at sigma = 0.78 pitch -- beyond it the checkerboard is lost even to calibration
=> blur is a concern only when its MTF at the DM's highest spatial frequency approaches the reconstruction's regularization/noise floor; below that a calibrated read removes it.
   the real tg96 leg (tg96_pupilsim) broadens a single poke by <~1% (width/true ~1.0) -- i.e. sigma ~ 0, the far-left of these curves.
