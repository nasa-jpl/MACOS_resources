---- the lesson ----
blur-free floor (sigma = 0, lambda 0.05, 20 pm): checker 3.511%, random 0.903% (all-unknowns control: 7.17% / 3.07%)
the checker error passes max(2 x floor, 1%): calibrated at sigma = 0.40 pitch, naive at sigma = 0.10 pitch
the structural knee: the stencil's Nyquist transfer (0.257 of its peak) x the blur MTF falls to lambda at sigma = 0.81 pitch -- beyond it the checkerboard is lost even to calibration
