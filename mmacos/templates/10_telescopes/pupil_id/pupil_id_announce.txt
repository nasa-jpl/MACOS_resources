Subject: New MACOS/mmacos capability -- exit-pupil identification beyond FEX

A new pupil-identification tool is now available in mmacos.  Give it a
telescope prescription and it characterizes the EXIT PUPIL as a fitted
surface -- and hands you back a revised Rx with an improved exit-pupil
reference sphere.

WHAT IT ADDS OVER FEX
  FEX ("find exit pupil") gives you a single conjugate sphere from ONE
  chief ray: the exit-pupil location and the far-field propagation radius,
  and nothing about pupil-imaging QUALITY.  This tool traces a grid of
  field cones (the "grid of cone sources") and finds where they converge,
  so it measures the exit pupil as an aberrated SURFACE across the field:

    - pupil aberration (defocus / astigmatism / coma / spherical),
    - departure from a perfect reference sphere (the pupil-quality number),
    - pupil-imaging blur and magnification / distortion,
    - PUPIL WALK vs field -- how the exit pupil moves as you scan the field,
      which a single-chief-ray FEX cannot show.

  It then writes a REVISED prescription in which the exit-pupil reference
  sphere is re-placed at the cone-bundle vertex (an improvement on FEX's
  one-ray vertex) while keeping FEX's far-field propagation radius -- so
  the deck stays correct for diffraction propagation.

  Cross-checked against the engine's own two-ray exit-pupil surface
  (XPS / pupil_quality): the two agree to a few tenths of a percent or
  better on the representative cases.

HOW TO RUN
  In MATLAB, with mmacos on the path:

    run_pupil_id('both')          % the two bundled example telescopes
    out = pupil_id('my_tel.in')   % your prescription -> my_tel_xp.in

  Any telescope Rx works; the exit-pupil element and stop are auto-detected
  (override with 'ep_elt' / 'xp_elt').  It emits a printed report, three
  figures (the convergence cloud + departure map, the aberration Zernikes
  vs the engine, and the pupil walk), and the revised prescription.

  Sensitivity/analysis drivers can call the core finder directly --
  pupil_find(rx, field_grid) -- which places the improved sphere into the
  loaded prescription in place, exactly the way FEX does.

WHERE IT LIVES
  mmacos/templates/10_telescopes/pupil_id/   (the run_pupil_id example)
  mmacos/design/src/pupil_find.m             (the reusable core finder)

  It composes existing, test-gated tools -- no engine change required.
