# `tma_unobscured/` — visible-band unobscured TMA: the coronagraph / imager / spectrometer front end

An eccentric-pupil (unobscured) three-mirror anastigmat for a **visible
telescope** — 500 nm center wavelength — built with the same
finder-then-demo approach as [`../tma_centered`](../tma_centered): a
constraint finder chooses the geometry against the packaging and
polarization rules, and the demo runs the visible correction ladder,
aligns the true focal plane, places the exit pupil, and emits the design
report.

The brief (Dave, 2026-07-06):

- **Slower M1** than the centered j18's f/1.2: a slower primary makes the
  section's per-mirror **AOI spread** gentler — the coronagraph
  polarization preference is **< 15° across the beam on every mirror** —
  and shrinks the clearing decenter. The price is tube length.
- **M2 close to the source→M1 beam** (launch-shroud packaging): the
  minimal-clearance eccentric section (`set_offaxis('all')`) *is* this
  rule — the decenter stops the moment every body clears with margin.
- The deliverable is a **front end**: unobscured pupil + accessible exit
  pupil (`PropType=FarField` — PSF/Strehl metrics live) for the
  coronagraph, and a flat, correctly-tilted focal plane for the imager
  and spectrometer pickoffs.

## The two scripts (run in this order)

| script | what it does |
|---|---|
| `tma_unobscured_search.m` | walks the **slower-M1 ladder** (primary f/1.5 → f/3.0), builds each candidate from the closed-form Korsch layout (`macos.design.tma_layout`), solves the conics, extracts the minimal unobscured section, and prints the clearance + AOI-spread + shroud trade table. The design point is **f/2.5** (Dave 2026-07-06) — comfortably under the AOI rule with margin for the coronagraph; verified MEETS and saved to `tma_unobscured_geometry.mat`. |
| `tma_unobscured.m` | rebuilds the winner and runs the visible ladder — [2a] as found → [2b] off-axis refigure → [2c] field balance → [2d] M1 stop-surface freeform → [2e] `align_focal_plane` (5×5 foci grid) — then the FOV ladder, clearance re-check, `add_pupil`, save, figures, and `design_report` → `tma_unobscured_report.txt`. |

Artifacts (`.in`, `.mat`, `.png`, `.txt`) are regenerated by the scripts
and git-ignored.

## Adapting to YOUR case

Same knob philosophy as `../tma_centered/README.md` (read its step-by-step
guide); the deltas here:

- **`LAM`** — 500 nm makes the DL bar 35 nm RMS: the same figure is 4.6×
  more waves than at 2.3 µm, which is why the ladder includes the M1
  stop-surface freeform stage by default.
- **`F1_STEPS`** — the slower-M1 ladder. Widen it if no step meets the
  AOI rule; the finder errors loudly rather than picking a violator.
- **`SYS_FNUM`** — sets plate scale and detector Nyquist (λF/2: f/20 at
  500 nm Nyquists ~5 µm pixels).
- **`AOI_MAX`** — the per-mirror AOI-spread preference (15°, coronagraph
  polarization rule; see `aoi_report`).
- **`SEC_MAG`, intermediate-focus placement** — `tma_layout` options
  (see `../tma_onaxis/README.md` for that designer).
