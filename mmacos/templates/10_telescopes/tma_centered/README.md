# `tma_centered/` — centered (obscured) Korsch TMA: the A/B answer + the buildable folded variant

The centered three-mirror anastigmat — concave M1, **convex** M2 on axis
(central obscuration accepted), concave M3 behind the primary — versus its
off-axis-section cousin, and then the packaging problem the coaxial form
hides: **the focal plane lands on the axis in the middle of the beam.**
This directory carries the full arc from parent choice to a buildable,
clearance-verified, focus/tilt-aligned folded design.

## The three scripts (run in this order)

| script | what it does |
|---|---|
| `tma_centered.m` | the **A/B study**: same j18 parent, centered vs off-axis section over a 5′-dia circular field — centered 0.065λ (symmetry intact) vs section 0.098λ (induced binodal field astig); + the conic field-ladder walls. Ends by showing the FP sits at z=−4.07 m, **inside the science beam**: unbuildable as derived. |
| `tma_centered_fold_search.m` | the **constraint finder**. Scans (M3 pushback) × (field bias), verifies every folded candidate with the full 3-D clearance judge, and saves the most compact compliant geometry to `tma_centered_fold_geometry.mat`. Run this FIRST. |
| `tma_centered_foldfp.m` | the **deliverable**: rebuilds the chosen geometry and runs the visible correction ladder ([2a] as-found → [2b] conics at the bias → [2c] M1 stop-surface freeform → [2d] true-FP align), the FOV ladder, the clearance verdict, and saves `.in`/`.mat` + all figures here. |

Artifacts (`.in`, `.mat`, `.png`) are regenerated by the scripts and
git-ignored; the scripts are the record.

## The extraction recipe (Dave, 2026-07-05)

1. **Field bias** (source tilt, `set_field_bias`) — the image walks EFL·θ off
   axis so the detector body can leave the science beam. 10′ clears an
   honest 0.25 m FP body here; 5′ does not.
2. **Flat fold in the M2→M3 FEED** (`add_fold`, WFE-neutral isometry), at a
   station behind the primary, turning the beam 90° into +x with the fold
   normal in the X-Z plane — so M3, the image, and the FP land on a **flat
   X-Y bench** behind the primary.
3. **M3 pushed back** — j18's own spacings put the exit pupil ~1 m in FRONT
   of the primary where no fold fits; +2 m opens the gap.

Then the performance ladder on the folded, biased geometry:

4. **Conics+ROC solved AT the bias field only** (`optimize` with
   `'fields_arcmin',[]`, DOFs `[0 0 0 0 0 0 1 1]`) — the annular-field
   anastigmat move: 30.6 → 0.061 waves @1 µm. Pointing DOFs stay OUT
   (they re-center the telescope into the bias and eat the fold clearance).
5. **Freeform on M1** (the stop — pupil-conjugate, field-neutral, fully
   lit): modes 5:15 → 0.046 @1 µm.
6. **`align_focal_plane`** — the TRUE focal plane of a biased field is
   TILTED wrt the chief ray; one focus point cannot identify it. Best-focus
   points are mapped on an N×N field grid (**2×2 for prelim analysis,
   5×5/7×7 for the final design**), the detector plane is fitted through
   them, and FP Vpt+psi are set from the fit. Here: tilt 7.32°, defocus
   removed 0.227 mm, residual field-curvature sag ±1.1 µm (≪ the f/20
   depth of focus — a flat detector is fine). Run BEFORE `add_pupil`.
7. `add_pupil` (exit-pupil reference — the EP sphere emits
   `PropType=FarField`, so `INT` at the detector is the far-field PSF),
   save, figures.
8. **`design_report`** — the one-page report written to
   `tma_centered_foldfp_report.txt`: EFL, f/# at M1 and at the FP, plate
   scale, λ/D, the WFE-vs-field ladder **with Strehl** (PSF peak /
   unaberrated-aperture peak, real far-field INT through the EP), FP
   tilt, exit-pupil handle, shroud/clearance/AOI.

Current result (D=6.605 m, f/20, 10′ bias, dM3=2 m, fold at z=0.87):

| ring about the bias | −tilt @1 µm | −tilt @2.3 µm |
|---|---|---|
| bias point | 0.046 | **0.020 — DL** |
| 0.25′ | 0.038 | **0.016 — DL** |
| 0.50′ | 0.042 | **0.018 — DL** |
| 1.00′ | 0.062 | **0.027 — DL** |
| 2.50′ | 0.230 | 0.100 |

(Judge blur on the −tilt column: raw at a ring field is the field-dependent
tilt a single-point solve leaves = distortion, calibratable.) Shroud
0.99×D; every body clear of every beam except M2's own accepted
obscuration; fold AOI 45° with 6.3° spread across the beam.

## Adapting this to YOUR case — step by step

All the knobs live in one block at the top of
`tma_centered_fold_search.m` (`USER DESIGN CHOICES`); the foldfp script
inherits everything through `tma_centered_fold_geometry.mat`.

**1. Parent prescription — `D`, `R`, `TBET`.** Three ways:
   - **You have a prescription** (this example: j18): set `R = [|R1| |R2| |R3|]`
     and `TBET = [t1 t2]` directly (magnitudes; the builder emits
     `KrElt=-|R|`, M2 `'convex',true`).
   - **You have f/#s**: `[R, t] = macos.design.tma_layout(D, primary_fnum,
     system_fnum, 'secondary_mag',m2, 'int_focus_m',z_int,
     'm3_behind_m',d3)` gives the closed-form Korsch layout from the
     primary f/# (R1 = 2·f1#·D), the system f/# (EFL = fsys#·D), and where
     you want the intermediate focus. See `../tma_onaxis/README.md` for
     that designer.
   - **Scale a validated design**: everything scales LINEARLY with D at
     fixed f/#s (multiply R, TBET, and the packaging dimensions by
     D_new/D_old). WFE in µm scales with D; in waves it scales with
     D/λ.
   - Rules of thumb: a **faster M1** (smaller primary f/#) shortens the
     tube but steepens the conics (alignment/figure sensitivity); the
     **system f/#** sets the plate scale and therefore the honest FP body
     radius (`EFL·θ_field + FP_HOUSING`) — a slower system needs MORE bias
     to walk that bigger body out of the beam.

**2. Wavelength — `LAM`.** Enters only the CALIB target and the
   waves-RMS yardsticks (geometry is achromatic, all-reflective); the
   scripts print both the design λ and the 2.3 µm yardstick — change
   either. Diffraction-limit threshold: 0.071 waves RMS (Maréchal).

**3. Science field — `FIELD_RAD`** (radius about the BIASED center,
   arcmin). Drives the honest FP body size and the FOV ladder. The
   extraction price grows ~(bias·Δfield) beyond conic reach, so keep the
   bias ladder tight (next knob).

**4. The scan ladders — `BIAS_STEPS`, `DM3_STEPS`.** The finder takes the
   LOWEST bias and the SHORTEST backbone that clear; widen the ladders if
   your case fails everywhere (fatter beams need more of both).

**5. Packaging realities** (all metres — resize to your hardware):
   - `FM_MARGIN` — fold mount margin beyond its beam footprint;
   - `FP_HOUSING` — detector housing radius beyond the image;
   - `ARM_MIN` — minimum fold→image arm (body-body room the beam-vs-body
     clearance judge cannot see);
   - `M1_KEEPOUT` — no fold body within this of the M1 vertex plane (the
     primary's support structure owns it);
   - `set_hole(M1, r)` — the perforated primary's through-hole radius.

**6. Sampling** — `'grid_npts'` (41 here; the align/ladder stages are
   grid-robust) and `'model_size'` (256).

**7. In `tma_centered_foldfp.m`** — the correction/alignment stages:
   `NGRID`/`SPAN` for the [2d] focal-surface map (**start 2×2 to see the
   tilt, refine 5×5 or 7×7 for the final design**), the freeform mirror +
   mode set in [2c] (keep it on the STOP surface; see the four printed
   lessons before moving it), and the optimizer iteration caps.

**8. Verify** (the scripts do all three — read their output, don't skip):
   the [4] clearance verdict (every body vs every beam + shroud), the FOV
   ladder against your requirement, and the saved `.in` traced standalone
   (`ray 1` in the macos CLI walks the chief through the full train
   including the FP→ExitPupil→FP pupil retrace).
