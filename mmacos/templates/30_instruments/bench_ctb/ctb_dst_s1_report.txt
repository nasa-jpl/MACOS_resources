==== ATTRIBUTION: why the N=512 baseline floored ~57x too high (2026-09-02) ====
The N=512 mono EFC stalled at ~4e-10 (half-plane relin), NOT decades below
DST -- the loop crawled at ~1%/iter, alpha pinned high, ~2 nm stroke, and
the stroke-released ctb_push found NO improving step (so it was not
stroke-starvation).  Parity gate: charge-4/Lyot-0.60/annulus reproduced
Session-12's 5.812e-13 to 3 digits -> machinery sound, no regression.
Attribution probes (statics only, matched config charge6/Lyot0.80):
  (i) mask supersample K=8/16/32 at N=512: static FLAT (3.293e-7 -> 3.205e-7,
      ~3%) though core |V| fell 2e-16 -> 9e-17.  Sub-pixel binning is NOT
      the lever (core already well-cancelled at K=8).
  (ii) N=1024 (K=8 or 16): static drops 57x (ann 3.293e-7 -> 5.764e-9;
      hp 1.059e-6 -> 1.866e-8).  The GRID is the lever -- the Session-11/12
      pattern (195x at charge 4; charge-6 phase wraps 1.5x faster at the
      core so its pixelated core at 2 px/lambda/D scatters more, and the
      0.80 Lyot passes precisely that core-diffracted residual a 0.50-0.60
      Lyot would eat).
VERDICT: the ~4e-10 N=512 floor was the coarse-grid pixelated vortex core,
a SAMPLING artifact -- NOT an open-Lyot controllability wall.  ctb_linfloor's
~1e-17 (N=512) was linear over-optimism: the core residual is coherent and
in the Jacobian's linear range, so the linear floor is optimistic while the
nonlinear loop stalls on the grid artifact.  At N=1024 the idealized floors
are ann 3.33e-14 / hp 2.04e-14 -- decades below DST (hp ~12000x below their
2.44e-10 mono), restoring the S1 premise.  BASELINE IS N=1024.  (The N=512
rows above are retained as the artifact record; do not use them as the
baseline.)
